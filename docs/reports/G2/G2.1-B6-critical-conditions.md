## 2. Plan: what I found

- **v3 items** (`records/life_safety_v3.go`) had no flag. v1/v2 records keep `critical_conditions` as plain labels, all from the onboarding opt-in.
- **Every doctor reader takes labels from one records function**, `records.ReadLifeSafety`:
  - the workspace (`workspace/service.go:218` → `EssentialInformationOf`, `workspace/model.go:50-59`);
  - the chart (`chartclinical/service.go:219` → `lifeSafetyBlock`).

  Both put **every** condition into `critical_conditions`.
- **The same function feeds G-1's consultation write** (`records/life_safety_apply.go:83`). That write reads the current record as labels and writes a **v2** record (no authors and no flag) on top of it.
- **No reader knows "full-passport consent".**
  - The workspace has per-section grant flags (`ESSENTIAL_INFORMATION`, `CURRENT_MEDICATIONS`, `CONSULTATION_SUMMARIES`).
  - The chart has a custodian mode plus grant sections (`chartclinical/service.go:159-170`).
  - So neither call site can tell "life-safety only" apart from "full consent" today.
- **Ownership** (roadmap §1.2): `workspace/` and the `records` consultation write path are G-1's. The chart code comes from G-1's commit `cd206f9`.

### 3c. The records helper G-1 needs (rule 2, ready)
`records.ReadLifeSafetyAlwaysVisible(schemaVersion, canonical)` is `ReadLifeSafety` for a professional read that is **not** under full-passport consent:
- blood type and allergies stay whole;
- only critical conditions are kept; v1/v2 conditions are all critical;
- if every condition is hidden, the status is **UNKNOWN**, never NONE_KNOWN, because « Aucun antécédent connu » would be false.

`ReadLifeSafety` itself is **unchanged**, so every current reader and G-1's consultation write behave exactly as before.

## 4. ⛔ STOP: what only G-1 can change

### 4.1 Why the filter cannot sit inside `ReadLifeSafety`
1. **It would lose data.** `ApplyConsultationMedicalSummary` (`records/life_safety_apply.go:83`, G-1's write path) reads through `ReadLifeSafety` and writes the result back. A filter there would **delete** the patient's non-critical conditions at the next consultation.
2. **It would hide too much.** The records layer does not know the doctor's access, so a filter there would hide non-critical conditions from a consented doctor too. The task's "a consented full read shows both" could not hold.
3. **There is nowhere to show them.** No doctor view has a field for non-critical conditions (`critical_conditions` only).

### 4.2 The exact change for G-1
1. **The consultation write must keep the v3 items and their flag (B4, P1, before anything below goes live).**
   - Today `ApplyConsultationMedicalSummary` writes **v2**, and on the next read its conditions all read as **critical**.
   - So every consultation that touches life-safety promotes the patient's non-critical conditions to "always visible", and drops item authors (already the known B4 issue).
   - Fix: merge on `records.LifeSafetyV3Of`, keep patient items with their `Critical`, and write v3 (or v4).
2. **The workspace** (`workspace/service.go:218`, the `ESSENTIAL_INFORMATION` block):
   - call `records.ReadLifeSafetyAlwaysVisible` instead of `records.ReadLifeSafety` when the access is **not** full-passport consent (§4.4);
   - with full consent, call `ReadLifeSafety` and send the non-critical conditions in a **new field** (for example `other_conditions`), never inside `critical_conditions`.
3. **The chart** (`chartclinical/service.go:180` → `lifeSafetyBlock` `:218-219`): the same rule, per `access.Mode` and `access.Sections`.
4. **Spring doctor DTOs and Angular** (G-1): carry and show the new field under the sharing rules.

### 4.3 What waits for 4.2 in G-2
- **Onboarding sends ALL conditions** (`critical = criticalOptIn` on new items). It is a 3-line change in `OnboardingService` case 3: remove the opt-in filter and pass each condition with its flag. The flag channel (`ConditionItemBody.critical`) is already in place.
- Until then, onboarding writes the opt-in conditions only, now with `critical=true`. Non-opt-in conditions are not saved (unchanged from SB2).
- The **editor (SB1) already saves non-critical conditions** (`critical=false`). Doctors still see them today, exactly as before B6: nothing is hidden yet, and nothing new is exposed.

### 4.4 Decision needed (CTO / G-1): what is "full-passport consent"?
- The workspace grants are per section, and the chart has custodian or granted modes. **Recommendation:** full consent = **custodian mode**, or a **grant whose policy authorizes every data section** (`ESSENTIAL_INFORMATION`, `CURRENT_MEDICATIONS`, `CONSULTATION_SUMMARIES`). Anything less = always-visible view.
- REQUIRES the CTO's confirmation; I did not decide it.
