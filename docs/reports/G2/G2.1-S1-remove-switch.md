## 6. Manual test steps for the CTO
Use a plain `flutter run -d chrome` with no `--dart-define`, against your local backend with the FT-1 code and MRS running.
1. Log in as a test patient who has at least one family member, then open « Dossier médical ».
2. The temporary sentence « Votre dossier médical n'est pas encore disponible… » never appears. You see the header (« Passeport médical · {your name} », with no pencil), then the Résumé / Historique / Documents tabs.
3. **Résumé:**
   - The identity card shows your name, phone and e-mail.
   - The essential info card shows your real blood type, allergies and conditions, or « Non renseigné ».
   - The treatments card and the measurements card show your real data, or their empty texts. There is never 180 cm / 78 kg unless that is your data.
4. Tap « Historique »: your consultations load (the first 20). Tap « Documents »: the empty documents state, as today (not wired yet, §4 item 2).
5. Tap your name and pick a family member. Every card briefly shows its loading state, then shows **their** data, never yours. Switch back to yourself.
6. Stop MRS and leave the screen. Come back, or switch member. Each card shows a French error and « Réessayer ». Start MRS and tap « Réessayer » on one card: only that card reloads.
7. Tap « Afficher le QR »: the secure QR opens. Close it. Tap the share icon: the sharing settings sheet opens. Close it.
8. (Android device only) On « Dossier médical », a screenshot is blocked. On the home screen, screenshots work again.
