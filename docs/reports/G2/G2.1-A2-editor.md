### 2.3 Findings that shaped the build

1. **Past declared medications would have been deleted.** The PUT replaces the patient's whole own list (SB1 §4a), and declared items move to the past group once their duration ends (B3 §5.2). The record only reads the first page of current treatments. The editor now reads every page of both groups (§3.3) and sends past own items back unchanged.
2. **A family member without an id reads as SELF.** `recordSubjectOf` sends such a member to SELF, which is fine for reads but wrong for a write: an edit would land on the account holder. The pencil is hidden for that member.
3. **No start-date field.** The task lists « start date » for medications, but the card has no date field, and adding one is a new control (roadmap §1.3). The stored date is kept and sent back unchanged (§6.1).
4. **No allergy preset list exists anywhere in the app**, only « Allergie Pénicilline » among the conditions. I did not invent a medical list (§6.2).
5. **ADR-012 §8 has no D6.** The prompt cites "ADR-012 D6" for "new patient conditions are not critical". That rule is the CTO decision of 2026-11-12 applied in B6/B6b, which is not recorded in the ADR. The app follows it (never sends `critical`). The ADR needs the entry (§6.5).

## 3. Decisions

1. **Pure logic, then the view model, then the screen.**
   - `medical_data_editor_draft.dart` (no Flutter widgets) holds the read baseline, the draft, local checks, change detection and request building.
   - `MedicalDataEditorViewModel` loads, saves and keeps the errors.
   - The screen only binds fields. Errors are kept as **API path + kind**, so a local check and a server refusal give the same French text (`recordFieldMessage`).
2. **Pencil and subject.** The pencil uses the subject of the person on screen; the editor view model is created for that subject and disposed when the editor closes. When it closes, the record reloads if **any** section saved (also after a partial failure, or a close with ✕), and a save warning shows once.
3. **Reading every medication page.**
   - `MedicalRecordRepository.editorMedications` walks `CURRENT` then `PAST` with `size=50` and the server's `nextCursor`.
   - The walk stops at **20 pages per group**. Past that, the read fails, rather than let a save drop items it never saw.
   - `listVersion` comes from the first page: if the list changes during the read, the save gets a 409.
   - The cards keep reading their first 20 items only.
4. **Change detection.**
   - **Blood type:** changed when a value is chosen and differs.
   - **Measurements:** changed when the text differs from the read value's text. So "70.25" shown as "70.3" isn't a change unless typed.
   - **Chips:** compared as sets of `{itemId, label, severity}`.
   - **Medications:** compared as the ordered list sent.
5. **One « Enregistrer ».**
   - **All checks first:** every changed section is checked on the device. If any field is refused, **nothing is sent**, and every refused field shows its message.
   - **Then in order:** sections are sent one by one (blood type, measurements, allergies, conditions, medications). A failure doesn't stop the next sections.
   - **After a partial save:** sections that saved are read back, so new items get their ids and are not sent again.
   - **« Réessayer »:** resends the remaining sections with the same keys (same content → same key, A1 `SaveAttempt`). An edit in a section drops its key and clears its messages.
6. **409.**
   - Only that section is read again; the draft on screen is never replaced.
   - Chips match the user's labels to the fresh own items by label, and a medication row whose item disappeared becomes a new item.
   - The conflict text shows; the next « Enregistrer » sends the draft at the new version, with a new key.
7. **Server field errors** land on the field: `items[n]` maps to the row that was sent at `n`. An error on a past item (no row) shows on the list.
8. **Blood type.**
   - **« Je ne sais pas »:** the read shows `UNKNOWN` as no value (Spring), so the editor shows « Je ne sais pas » when the section has a version but no value.
   - **Never recorded:** shows « Non renseigné ».
   - **No default:** nothing is sent until a value is chosen.
   - **Locked:** the field is disabled when the author is a professional (or unknown, with a version).
9. **Measurements.** Both are optional. Clearing a **recorded** value is refused with « Ce champ est obligatoire. », because the save can't erase a value: the server keeps what it has. A comma is accepted as the decimal sign.
10. **IMC.**
    - The number and the pill come from `displayedBmi` (one decimal) and `bmiCategoryOf`, in `medical_reference_values.dart`, marked REQUIRES MEDICAL VALIDATION.
    - The category is taken from the number shown, so it always matches.
    - No height or weight: no number, no pill text, no marker.
    - The pill keeps its existing look; only its text is computed.
11. **Allergies (D2).**
    - The chip widget now takes its title, presets and hint as parameters, and is used for both lists.
    - « Allergie Pénicilline » moved from the condition presets to the allergy presets (one constants file).
    - Severity has no control. An own allergy keeps its stored severity; a new one has none.
12. **Medications.**
    - The name is a plain text field (no catalog).
    - Frequency uses the existing 1×/2×/3× chips. A stored value of 4–6 is kept until a chip is tapped, and `null` stays `null`.
    - A new row's duration is empty (hint « 0 »). « 0 » typed is refused (« Entre 1 et 365. »).
    - Every editable row shows « Déclaré par le patient » (the existing card's label style). The Résumé row shows it under the dose line for any `PATIENT_DECLARED` item.
13. **No value in logs.** The editor logs nothing. Every draft, input, baseline and error `toString` carries no label or name, and failures carry codes only (tested).

## 6. Open points and decisions for the CTO

1. **Medication start date.**
   - **Today:** no field exists. The stored date is kept and resent unchanged, and a new medication has none.
   - **To make it editable:** a date field needs a Figma design (roadmap §1.3).
   - **Your call:** add the field (design first), or accept "no start date" in V1.
2. **Allergy presets.** The list holds only « Allergie Pénicilline » (moved, D2). A doctor should provide the list, along with the condition presets and the BMI thresholds (all in `medical_reference_values.dart`, REQUIRES MEDICAL VALIDATION).
3. **No way to record « Aucune allergie » from « Non renseigné » in the editor.** Clearing the chips of a list that has own items records « Aucune » (rule 4). A list that was never filled stays « Non renseigné », because an untouched section is not sent. Onboarding and Ma Santé can still record it. If the editor needs it, that's a design question (a new control).
4. **Clearing a recorded height or weight is refused** (« Ce champ est obligatoire. »), because the API can't erase a measurement. A "remove measurement" feature would be a backend change.
5. **ADR-012 D6** isn't written in the ADR. Please record the 2026-11-12 decision: per-condition `critical`, new patient conditions not critical, onboarding opt-in as the flag.
6. **Text checks without NFC.** Dart has no built-in NFC, so the app's 160-character count and duplicate check work on the text as typed. A decomposed accent may count twice on the device. The server (NFC) stays the authority, and its answer lands on the field.
7. **Release blocker carried from B6/B6b:** the doctor views must hide non-critical conditions before any real patient (G-1).

## 7. Manual test steps for the CTO

Run `flutter run -d chrome --web-port=53472 --dart-define=OMNICARE_API_BASE_URL=http://localhost:8080`, with Spring and MRS (Docker) running. Log in with a test account that has at least one family member.

4. **Allergies.**
   - The new « Allergies » section shows « Allergie Pénicilline ». Tap it, type « Lactose » in « Autre allergie... » and tap « Ajouter ». Save: the Résumé shows both.

   - Save: the Résumé « Actuels » list shows it, with « Déclaré par le patient » under the dose line.
   - Reopen, change the dose, and save: it updates. Reopen, tap « Supprimer ce médicament », and save: it's gone.
7. **Wrong values: each shows its French text, nothing is saved, nothing typed is lost.**

   - Poids 600 → « Le poids doit être compris entre 0 et 500 kg. »
   - Taille « abc » → « Format invalide. »

   - Durée 0 → « Entre 1 et 365. »
   - Two medications named « Doliprane » → « Cet élément figure déjà dans la liste. » on the second
   - Adding « asthme » when « Asthme » is selected keeps one chip
   - A 161-character allergy → « 160 caractères maximum. »
8. **Family member.**
   - Tap the name in the header and pick a family member: the pencil is there and the title shows their first name. Change their blood type and add a condition, then save: their Résumé changes.
   - Switch back to yourself: your data is unchanged.
